DATA ATTRIBUTION
================

The dataset in CST.zip is derived from WikiConv:

    Yiqing Hua, Cristian Danescu-Niculescu-Mizil, Dario Taraborelli,
    Nithum Thain, Jeffery Sorensen and Lucas Dixon.
    "WikiConv: A Corpus of the Complete Conversational History of a Large
    Online Collaborative Community."
    EMNLP 2018.

Greek and Chinese Wikipedia releases, both distributed under
Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0).

The conversation structure used as the labels is the corpus's own reply graph,
reconstructed by its authors from the talk-page revision history. It is not a
rater's opinion and not a model's output.

Comment text is Wikipedia editor content, licensed
Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike (CC BY-SA).
    https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

Editor handles have been replaced in the message text by the token <name>, and
IP addresses by <ip>. Author identity, indentation and section headings are not
present in the files.

This repository redistributes the derived files unmodified from the challenge
archive, for the purpose of reproducing the solution in solution.py.
